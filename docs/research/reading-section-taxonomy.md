# Professional Astrology Reading — Section & Topic Taxonomy

A comprehensive inventory of the sections, topics, and interpretive moves that credentialed astrologers cover in a full consultation, drawn from accredited association syllabi, certification competencies, recognized school curricula, and named authorities. For the GraveSigns product, items especially relevant to a **single-moment / death-transition chart** are flagged **[MOMENT]**; items that presuppose a whole biography are flagged **[NATAL-ONLY]**.

> Compiled by web research restricted to gold-standard / accredited sources (NCGR, ISAR/CAPISAR, AFA, OPA, the Faculty of Astrological Studies, Kepler College, and named authorities). See "Sources Consulted" at the end.

---

## 1. Macro Structure / Arc of a Professional Consultation

The recognized consulting-skills bodies teach a reading as a *shaped arc*, not a placement dump. The dominant model is "client-centered vs. information-centered" modes that the astrologer alternates between.

- **Pre-session preparation** — reviewing the chart in advance, identifying dominant signatures and repeated testimonies before the client arrives; the astrologer's own chart/synastry awareness with the client.
- **Opening / intention-setting** — establishing rapport, surfacing the client's questions and reason for coming, framing what astrology can and cannot do.
- **Chart overview / gestalt first** — leading with the whole-chart picture before details, so the reading feels coherent rather than fragmented.
- **Thematic body** — the substance, organized around themes and life areas rather than a mechanical planet-by-planet walk.
- **Synthesis** — weaving factors into a coherent story; explicitly named as "the art of synthesis" and treated as the core professional skill.
- **Timing / forecast segment** — placing the client's present and near-future in a predictive frame.
- **Closing / integration / aftercare** — summarizing, answering remaining questions, leaving the client with actionable meaning and next steps.
- **Consultation-craft skills threaded throughout** — effective listening, ethical boundaries, unconscious bias, managing the astrologer's own presence, alternating client-centered and information-centered modes.

**Sources:** OPA Consulting Skills Program (effective listening, ethical boundaries, unconscious bias, astrologer's own chart influence); ISAR CAPISAR "compassionate consulting" component and Consulting Skills Training (client-centered vs. information-centered modes); Faculty of Astrological Studies Module 9 "Astro-Consultancy" (live client reading, ethics, self-awareness via astrologer–client synastry); OPA "Common Challenges in Astrology Consultations" (synthesis vs. fragmented planet-by-planet delivery); NCGR-PAA Level IV consulting specialization.

## 2. Full Interpretive Inventory — Every Chart Factor a Pro Addresses

Grouped from foundational to advanced, matching the progression taught across NCGR Levels I–IV, the ISAR competency areas, AFA exam levels, and the Faculty foundation-to-diploma sequence.

### 2a. Chart-as-Whole / Gestalt (the "overview" layer)
- **Chart shape / planetary pattern** (bundle, bowl, bucket, seesaw, splash, locomotive, etc.) **[MOMENT]**
- **Hemispheric emphasis** (north/south, east/west) **[MOMENT]**
- **Quadrant emphasis** **[MOMENT]**
- **Element balance** (fire/earth/air/water) and any missing/overloaded element **[MOMENT]**
- **Modality / quadruplicity balance** (cardinal/fixed/mutable) **[MOMENT]**
- **Overall temperament / dominant signature** synthesized from the above **[MOMENT]**

**Sources:** NCGR Level I ("hemisphere and quadrant emphases … and other fundamentals"); Kepler College Natal Fundamentals; OPA emphasis on leading with chart gestalt.

### 2b. The Foundational Building Blocks
- **The twelve signs** — as planetary filters, not standalone **[MOMENT]**
- **The twelve houses** — life-area fields **[MOMENT]**
- **The planets** (Sun through Pluto) by sign, by house, and by aspect **[MOMENT]**
- **Personal points / sensitive points** — Ascendant, Midheaven, Descendant, IC (the four angles) **[MOMENT]**
- **Major aspects** (conjunction, opposition, trine, square, sextile) and orbs **[MOMENT]**

**Sources:** NCGR Level I ("signs, planets, houses, personal points, major aspects and their configurations"); AFA natal delineation; Faculty Foundation Course; Kepler Natal Fundamentals.

### 2c. The Luminaries and the "Big Three"
- **The Sun** — core identity, vitality, purpose **[MOMENT]**
- **The Moon** — emotional nature, needs, instinct, nurture **[MOMENT]**
- **The Ascendant / rising sign** and its **chart ruler** (ruler of the Ascendant by sign, house, aspect — a primary interpretive thread) **[MOMENT]**
- **Sun–Moon–Ascendant synthesis** as the identity spine of the reading **[MOMENT]**

**Sources:** Kepler Natal Fundamentals; evolutionary-astrology "Sun = identity/authenticity, Moon = needs/wellbeing" questions (Steven Forrest); Faculty intermediate interpretation.

### 2d. Angles and the Axes
- **Ascendant–Descendant axis** (self vs. other) **[MOMENT]**
- **MC–IC axis** (vocation/public vs. roots/private) **[MOMENT]**
- **Planets conjunct the angles** (angular emphasis) **[MOMENT]**
- **Vertex / anti-vertex** and **equatorial ascendant (East Point)** **[MOMENT]**

**Sources:** NCGR Level II (required calculation of "the vertex … and equatorial ascendant"); AFA.

### 2e. Aspect Patterns / Configurations

## 3. Life-Area / Topical Sections a Client Expects

These are the "chapters" a client experiences the reading as, that pros build by synthesizing the factors above onto the houses/planets governing each domain.

- **Identity, character & life purpose** (Sun / Ascendant / chart ruler / MC) **[MOMENT]**
- **Emotional nature & inner life** (Moon / IC / 4th house / water houses) **[MOMENT]**
- **Vocation, career & public role** (MC, 10th, 6th, 2nd; Saturn, Sun) — a topic AFA explicitly tests
- **Money, resources & values** (2nd/8th houses, Venus, Jupiter) — AFA "finance"
- **Relationships & partnership** (Venus, Mars, 7th house, Descendant, synastry) — ISAR & AFA both test relationship/synastry
- **Family, roots & ancestry** (4th/IC, Moon, Saturn) — ISAR "family patterns"; AFA "family"
- **Children & creativity** (5th house, Sun, Jupiter)
- **Health & vitality** (6th, 1st, Sun/Moon condition, Mars, medical astrology) — AFA medical/health strand
- **Communication, mind & learning** (Mercury, 3rd/9th)
- **Belief, philosophy, travel & higher meaning** (9th, Jupiter)
- **Community, friendship & aspirations** (11th house)
- **Spirituality, karma, soul purpose & the unconscious** (12th, Neptune, Pluto, nodes) **[MOMENT — central to a death/transition chart]**
- **Crisis, transformation, death & rebirth themes** (8th house, Pluto, Saturn, Mars) **[MOMENT — the axis most directly activated in a death chart]**

**Sources:** ISAR CAPISAR competency areas (synastry, family patterns, relationship & group dynamics); AFA professional exam (explicit "finance, career, family" delineation questions plus medical/mundane specialty exams); Faculty Level 2 (relationship astrology, family astrology); evolutionary astrology (soul purpose / 12th–Pluto themes).

## 4. Predictive / Timing Layer

The accredited "forecasting toolkit," taught as a required competency block by every body surveyed.

- **Transits** (outer-planet transits to natal, ongoing cycles) **[MOMENT — a transition chart *is* essentially a transit snapshot]**
- **Secondary progressions** (progressed Sun, Moon, planets; progressed lunation cycle) **[NATAL-ONLY]**
- **Solar arc directions** **[NATAL-ONLY]**
- **Solar return charts** (annual) and **lunar returns** **[NATAL-ONLY]**
- **Eclipses and lunations** as timing triggers **[MOMENT]**
- **Annual profections** (traditional time-lord technique)
- **Primary directions** (classical)
- **Firdaria / other time-lord systems** (traditional)
- **Planetary cycles & returns** (Saturn return, Jupiter return, etc.) **[NATAL-ONLY]**
- **Rectification** (deriving/verifying birth time — a Level IV / professional skill) **[NATAL-ONLY]**

**Sources:** ISAR CAPISAR competency ("forecasting skills including secondary progressions, return charts, transits, eclipses and lunations"); NCGR Level II (transits, progressions, solar arc directions); AFA professional (progressed charts, solar returns, solar arc, rectification); Faculty Modules 4–5 ("Time I – Transits and Solar Returns," "Time II – Progressions and Directions"); NCGR Level IV rectification essay.

## 5. Synthesis / Meaning-Making Moves

How professionals turn a list of placements into a coherent reading — explicitly taught as the distinguishing skill of a competent astrologer.

- **Identify repeated testimonies / dominant signatures** — themes echoed by multiple independent factors (a planet, a house, a sign, an aspect all "saying the same thing"). **[MOMENT]**
- **Lead with the gestalt, then support with detail** — top-down rather than fragment-up. **[MOMENT]**
- **Weave into themes, not a planet-by-planet list** — explicitly warned against as "fragmented and contradictory." **[MOMENT]**
- **Reconcile contradictions** — holding tension between conflicting placements as part of the person's/moment's complexity. **[MOMENT]**
- **Archetypal / question-based interpretation** — treating each planet as posing a developmental question the person answers consciously or unconsciously (evolutionary method). **[MOMENT]**
- **Prioritize / triage** — deciding what matters most for *this* client/moment and what to leave out. **[MOMENT]**
- **Narrative framing** — the chart as a "symbolic story" (past conditioning → present intention → future direction). **[MOMENT — maps naturally onto a death/transition arc]**
- **Client-centered tailoring** — bending emphasis toward the client's stated questions and lived reality.

**Sources:** OPA "Common Challenges in Astrology Consultations" (synthesis as core art; avoid fragmented planet-by-planet); ISAR CAPISAR essay competency ("critical thinking and interpretive skills … synthesize quickly"); evolutionary astrology (planets-as-questions, narrative story method — Steven Forrest); Faculty Module 6/9 ("integrate the wide range of skills … into an advanced chart interpretation").

## 6. Consultation Craft Sections (Non-"Astrology" but Part of the Reading)

Formal competency areas that accredited bodies require *independently* of chart knowledge.

- **Setting intention & surfacing the client's questions** — the reading is oriented to why the client came.
- **Ethical framing / informed consent** — scope of practice, what astrology claims, avoiding deterministic overreach.
- **Handling sensitive/difficult material** — death, illness, crisis, loss — delivered with care and without harm. **[MOMENT — directly load-bearing for a death chart]**
- **Boundaries & scope** — knowing when to refer out (therapy, medical, legal), confidentiality.
- **Managing prediction responsibly** — framing timing as potential/opportunity rather than fixed fate.
- **Unconscious bias & self-awareness** — the astrologer monitoring their own chart/reactions in the room.
- **Effective/active listening** — client-centered mode.
- **Closing, integration & aftercare** — leaving the client with usable meaning, summary, and next steps.
- **Ethics as a graded, formal requirement** — tested/awarded on its own.

**Sources:** ISAR CAPISAR (three pillars: personal ethics, astrological competency, compassionate consulting; separate Ethics Awareness Training exam at 80%+); OPA Consulting Skills Program (effective listening, ethical boundaries, unconscious bias, astrologer's chart influence) and OPA Certification (ethics, consulting skills, business/practice); NCGR ethics graded at all four levels; Faculty Module 9 (ethical considerations, self-awareness via astrologer–client synastry); AFA and OPA professional-conduct standards.

## Notes for the GraveSigns Product (Single-Moment / Death-Transition Chart)

- **Most transferable to a moment chart:** everything in §2a–2j (the entire static interpretive inventory works on any timed chart), the Moon's phase, the nodal/karmic axis, the 8th-house/Pluto/Saturn "death–transformation" complex, fixed stars, lots, and the *transit* portion of §4.
- **Least applicable:** techniques that require a life to unfold over time — **secondary progressions, solar arc, solar/lunar returns, planetary returns, and rectification** (§4, NATAL-ONLY) — because they presuppose a birth and a subsequent biography rather than a single sealed moment.
- **The narrative/synthesis frame (§5) and the sensitive-material craft (§6) are unusually important** for a death chart: the evolutionary "past conditioning → present intention → onward direction" story maps almost one-to-one onto a transition/threshold reading, and the ethical handling of death is a named professional competency rather than an optional nicety.
